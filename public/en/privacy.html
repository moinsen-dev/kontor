Privacy

This is an English translation. The legally binding version is the German Datenschutzerklärung.

In short: the survey is anonymous. We store no names, no e-mail addresses and no IP addresses, set no cookies ourselves and embed no third-party services such as fonts, analytics or advertising.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, acting as our processor. When you visit, Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not analyse this connection data ourselves and have switched off application logging.

To protect against automated access, Cloudflare adds a small check script to every page, loaded from this address, and may set a technically necessary security cookie (“cf_clearance”) for it. It is used only to tell bots from humans, not for analytics or advertising. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Survey

When you submit the survey, we store your selected answers, your optional free text, the time, and whether you used the German or English page. The data is kept in a Cloudflare database (Cloudflare D1, located in Western Europe). It is not linked to any person. Please don’t write names or other personal details in the free-text fields; if you do, we remove them during the analysis.

The legal basis is your consent by submitting (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The purpose is to find out what bothers people about professional networks and what they want instead. The aggregated results are publicly visible on this site; free text is never published verbatim. We delete individual answers once the survey has been analysed, at the latest on 31 December 2027.

Browser storage

After you submit, your browser keeps only the value “already took part” in local storage (localStorage), so the survey isn’t shown to you again. This value is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and withdrawal of consent (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Because answers can’t be linked to a person, we usually cannot find individual answers. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
